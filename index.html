Messiaen's 7 Modes of Limited Transposition

Formulated by Olivier Messiaen in Technique de mon langage musical (1944) and explored extensively by guitarists like Allan Holdsworth (via Nicolas Slonimsky's Thesaurus), these symmetrical pitch collections repeat after only a few transpositions due to their internal intervallic symmetry.

Mode 1 (Whole-Tone): 2 transpositions • [C, D, E, F#, G#, A#]

Symmetrical by whole steps. Creates pure augmented triads and dominant 7b5/7#5 soundscapes.

Mode 2 (Half-Whole Diminished / Octatonic): 3 transpositions • [C, Db, Eb, E, F#, G, A, Bb]

Symmetrical in minor 3rds. Rich in minor 7th, half-diminished, and altered dominant options.

Mode 3: 4 transpositions • [C, D, Eb, E, F#, G, Ab, Bb, B]

Symmetrical in major 3rds (2-1-1 pattern). Yields lush Maj7#11, min9, and augmented structures.

Mode 4: 6 transpositions • [C, Db, D, F, F#, G, Ab, B]

Symmetrical at the tritone. Features Dbmaj7#11, G7b9#11, and angular minor 7b5 colors.

Mode 5: 6 transpositions • [C, Db, F, F#, G, B]

Symmetrical at the tritone (1-4-1 pattern). Distinctive wide tritone-based sparse harmony.

Mode 6: 6 transpositions • [C, D, E, F, F#, G#, Bb, B]

Symmetrical at the tritone (2-2-1-1 pattern). Yields Cmaj7#11, Bb7alt, and augmented triads.

Mode 7: 6 transpositions • [C, Db, D, Eb, F, F#, G, Ab, A, B]

10-Note symmetrical scale (1-1-1-2 pattern). Almost chromatic with rich modal choices.
